              06Data feed

              Every number on this page is fetched live from two public APIs and worked out in your browser. No server, no database, no framework.

              • Throttled per APIEach source has its own rate-limited queue, so the page stays inside free-tier limits.
              • Retries, then fallbacksFailed calls back off and retry, then use an older cached copy, then a nightly snapshot.
              • Cached on your deviceResponses go into the browser cache, so a second visit loads almost instantly.
              • Joined across sourcesJolpica knows drivers by code, OpenF1 by car number. The page matches them up.
              • Derived, not downloadedRunning order, gaps, ideal laps and title maths are computed from raw timing data.
